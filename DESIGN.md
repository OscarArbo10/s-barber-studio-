## Brand & Style
The design system embodies the intersection of high-end editorial luxury and street-level precision barbering. It speaks to an audience seeking unapologetic mastery, technical craft, and modern grooming culture. The emotional response is focused, confident, and tactile—reminiscent of bespoke tailoring, matte steel tools, and razor-sharp line-ups.

The aesthetic fuses **High-Contrast Minimalism** with **Neo-Brutalist Structuralism** and modern dark-mode luxury:
- Monolithic silhouettes punctuated by razor-thin borders.
- Aggressive, rhythm-shifting typographic hierarchy using condensed/geometric display styles juxtaposed against hyper-legible body mechanics.
- Tactile, mechanical interactions featuring quick, snappy transitions (150ms cubic-bezier) that mimic the click of motorized clippers and precision shears.
- Dynamic masonry and staggered showcase grids to exhibit texture, skin fades, and craft without editorial clatter.

## Colors
The palette is hyper-monochromatic, structured around deep obsidian blacks, carbon midtones, industrial chrome slates, and optic white.

- **Primary (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure optic white reserved for primary interactive states, key actions, high-impact titles, and focal points.
- **Secondary (`#71717A`)**: Anodized zinc tone used for secondary meta-information, inactive steps, structural dividers, and subtle iconographic accents.
- **Tertiary (`#A1A1AA`)**: Polished silver for readable metadata, hover outlines, and micro-labels.
- **Neutral (`#09090B`)**: Deepest void black, providing the infinite depth base canvas, paired with step-up container tones (`#121215`, `#18181B`, `#27272A`) for dimensional layering.

Functional accents remain strictly disciplined:
- **Success/Available**: `#E4E4E7` (White-zinc luminescence) or an austere optic emerald `#10B981` used strictly for active booking status pips.
- **Critical/Destructive**: High-voltage crimson `#EF4444`, used sparingly on error surfaces.

## Typography
Typographic expression hinges on deliberate tension between the commanding, architectural weight of **Syne** (set in high-impact title casing or aggressive full-caps for service names and editorial section headers) and the ergonomic precision of **Plus Jakarta Sans** and **Inter**.

- **Editorial Headings (`Syne`)**: Used for branding, category anchors, and hero statements. Tracking is clamped tightly (`-0.04em` to `-0.02em`) to produce a monolithic street-poster punch.
- **Content Interface (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Delivers supreme readability for service descriptions, barber bios, pricing structures, and long-form narrative.
- **Mechanical Labels & UI Data (`Inter`)**: Rendered in uppercase with generous tracking (`0.06em` to `0.12em`) for slot times, operational tags, category badges, and technical specs.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout strategy employs a **Fluid-Responsive 12-Column Grid** anchored to a baseline 4px/8px metric rhythm. Content architecture breaks standard corporate symmetry by mixing structural full-bleed modules with staggered asymmetrical columns for cut galleries, barber rosters, and appointment funnels.

- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12 columns, 24px gutters, 48px outer canvas margins. Maximalist visual space allows dramatic scale shifts between bold hero headers and condensed booking matrices.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8 columns, 20px gutters, 32px margins. Two-column booking views collapse into clean stacked drawer patterns.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, 16px gutters, 20px margins. Galleries convert to smooth horizontal swipe-snaps with peek previews. Fixed bottom-anchored booking action bars ensure immediate conversion access.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is built through **Dark Surface Stratification** and **Precision Razor Edges** rather than soft diffuse drop shadows:

- **Canvas Base (`#09090B`)**: Ground zero. Pure structural background.
- **Surface Level 1 (`#121215`)**: Structural panels, section backings, and inert cards bordered by `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Surface Level 2 (`#18181B`)**: Interactive elements, calendar day matrices, selectable service modules. Border transitions to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16)` on hover.
- **Surface Level 3 / Floating Panels (`#27272A`)**: Modals, slide-out cart drawers, elevated tooltips. Supported by high-density backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`) and a sharp, directional edge highlight: `0 0 0 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.18), 0 20px 40px -10px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8)`.
- **Focus / Active Glow**: Rather than neon halos, selected states use a clean, razor-thin optic white ring: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px #FFFFFF, inset 0 0 0 1px #FFFFFF`.

## Shapes
The shape language is strictly **Soft Architectural (Level 1)**: crisp, disciplined, and utilitarian.
- Standard interactive elements, service cards, inputs, and modal shells utilize `0.25rem` (4px) to `0.5rem` (8px) corners, retaining a sharp, chiselled tool-grade aesthetic.
- Category filters, service duration tags, and VIP status indicators break the grid as explicit structural **pills** (`rounded-full` / 9999px) to provide direct visual contrast against angular frame architecture.
- Media frames maintain zero radius or minimal 4px corners to celebrate raw hair texture and clean fade photography without softening editorial impact.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Solid `#FFFFFF` fill with `#09090B` bold typography (`label-lg`). High-speed inverse transition on hover (`#E4E4E7` background with slight upward translate `-1px`).
- **Secondary Action**: Bordered button (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`), dark fill (`#121215`), white text. On hover, background shifts to `#18181B` and border lightens to pure `#FFFFFF`.
- **Ghost/Tertiary**: No border, monochrome slate text (`#A1A1AA`), shifts to pure white with animated bottom razor stroke.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Pills**: Pill-shaped capsules (`rounded-full`), dark translucent backings (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`), featuring an 8px circular status indicator dot (e.g., solid optic white for "Available Today", muted slate for "Booked Out").
- **Meta Chips**: Fixed 24px height, uppercase `label-sm`, hairline borders.

### Booking & Schedule Cards
- **Service Selection Cards**: Built on Surface Level 1. Left border displays an optional 2px indicator bar for selected states. Includes clear hierarchical typography: Service title (`title-md`), duration badge (`label-sm`), and optic white price callout (`title-lg`).
- **Barber Profile Cards**: Dramatic portrait container with a subtle bottom-gradient overlay (`from-black/90 to-transparent`) anchoring the barber's handle, specialty tags, and direct "Book Chair" trigger.

### Calendar & Time Slots
- **Date Selector**: Horizontal day-picker with monospaced numerical day labels and micro day-of-week headers. Active date reverses to solid white tile with black text.
- **Time Chips**: Grid of structured rectangular chips (`rounded-sm`). Available times render in sharp white text with faint slate borders; unavailable slots display strikethrough styling with `#3F3F46` text.

### Form Inputs
- **Text & Select Fields**: Dark slate backgrounds (`#121215`), 1px low-contrast border (`#27272A`), zero ambient shadow. On focus, the border turns optic white with zero fuzzy glow, complemented by a persistent floating uppercase label (`label-sm`).

### Lists & Navigation
- **Roster & Pricing Tables**: Strict edge-to-edge dividing rules (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`). Rows expand on hover with a subtle background shift to `#18181B` and an exposed right-aligned booking arrow icon.
